How it works

Designed for real minds.

Daily Rhythm ®

Your day, at a glance.

Kinda learns your rhythm and greets you with exactly what matters — no noise, no overwhelm. Just a clear picture of your day.

Instant Capture ®

Add it your way.

Voice note, quick type, or paste a link. Kinda captures whatever's on your mind the moment it hits — no format required.

AI Co-pilot ®

AI plans the path.

Kinda breaks your task into one clear first step. Starting never feels overwhelming when the AI has already done the thinking.

Momentum ®

Keep the momentum.

Check it off and Kinda queues up the next step. Each small win builds toward getting things done — for real this time.

Self-Compassion ®

Celebrate your wins.

Kinda reminds you to pause and appreciate your progress—building self-compassion instead of psychological pressure.

Weekly Insights ®

Reflect and grow.

Review simple weekly insights to see when your mind is naturally focused and when it needs restorative rest.
